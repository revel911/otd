PadLock

Drums that listen to your riff.

Plug in. Play. PadLock hears your guitar or bass and writes a drum part that locks to it. Shape each section with a few knobs, audition it, then drag the MIDI into your DAW. Windows and Mac, no plugin install.

  • Locks the kick to your playing, not to a grid.
  • Thinks in songs: fills, transitions and entries planned across the whole arrangement.
  • Speaks GM, Superior Drummer 3, EZdrummer 3, GetGood Drums and KRIMH.
Visit drumwithpadlock.com

About

A studio that took a gap year. Fifteen of them.

The studio

Our Time Designs opened in 2008 to help Richmond’s small businesses look good on the brand-new smartphone screen. Ad agencies, magazines, construction companies: if it needed design and marketing, we did it.

Then the founder went to lead design at a very large bank, and the studio went quiet. In 2026 it’s back with a sharper job: build our own products, and ship them.

The founder

Tommy Rayburn has led design for twenty years, the last thirteen at Capital One, where he runs design for AI and machine-learning platforms. Before that: small business banking, credit card rewards used by tens of millions of people, board games, VR.

He also plays. PadLock exists because he got tired of programming drums by hand.
