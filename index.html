PadLock

Adaptive drums for guitarists and bassists.

Plug in your guitar or bass, play the riff, and PadLock writes a drum part that locks to it. Shape each song section with a few knobs, audition it, then drag the MIDI straight into your DAW. A standalone app for Windows and Mac.

  • Hears the onsets in your playing and lands the kick on them.
  • Whole songs, section by section, with fills and transitions planned across the arrangement.
  • Note maps for GM, Superior Drummer 3, EZdrummer 3, GetGood Drums and KRIMH.
Visit drumwithpadlock.com

About

A studio, a founder, and a long pause in between.

The studio

Our Time Designs opened in Richmond in 2008, when smartphones were new and small businesses needed someone to help them show up there. For three years we did design and marketing for ad agencies, magazines, construction firms and other local companies.

Then the founder went to lead design inside a large bank, and the studio went quiet. In 2026 it came back with a different job: building and shipping our own products, with the same care we used to put into other people’s.

The founder

Tommy Rayburn has spent twenty years in design, the last thirteen at Capital One, where he currently leads design for AI and machine-learning platforms and previously set the experience direction for small business banking and consumer credit card rewards.

Outside of banking he has designed and produced board games, consulted on VR interaction design, and played music for most of his life. PadLock exists because he wanted it to.
